# Sources and reuse review — 2026-10-31

## Interactive T7 landmark sources

The two supplied AnatomyLearning screenshots were reviewed for interaction/layout only. No website model, marker dataset, script, texture, definition or brand asset was acquired. Gross-feature descriptions reference the consulted public-domain English **Gray (1918)** chapters [General Characteristics of a Vertebra](https://anatomie.cosminpopa.ro/en/3a-general-characteristics-of-a-vertebra/) (Body, Pedicles, Laminae, Processes) and [The Thoracic Vertebrae](https://anatomie.cosminpopa.ro/en/3a-2-the-thoracic-vertebr/) (typical middle-thoracic morphology). The pages expressly distinguish public-domain English text from copyrighted modern translation. Original brief explanations only; no illustrations or translation reused. These historical morphology references do not establish current clinical guidance.

Actual six-point positions were independently identified on the existing BodyParts3D FMA9968 mesh and checked in original multi-view renders. Mesh-derived coordinates retain CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan attribution/share-alike in their binding JSON and public audit export. Anatomy identity and geometric review remain separate from independent medical approval, which is pending. NCBI direct page access challenged this session and was not bypassed; no protected data corpus was imported. See LANDMARK_SYSTEM.md.

Original factual explanations, including expanded partial bone drafts, and individual terminology terms are stored in the application. No Complete Anatomy descriptions, screenshots, thumbnails, audio or medical datasets feed the app.

## Geometry candidates and chosen content

BodyParts3D remains the main source: 1,169 anatomical entries, individually selectable FMA-labelled surfaces, 99%-reduced source OBJ converted to indexed GLB. Licensed source terms and per-file hashes remain in the existing manifest. Source reduction limits fine detail; textures/clinical segmentations are absent. This content supports commercial redistribution under the embedded CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan conditions, with attribution and share-alike retained.

Z-Anatomy was evaluated per scope rather than treating all repository content as uniformly reusable. Its [official License.txt](https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/blob/master/License.txt) licenses its content CC BY-SA 4.0 and separately credits noncommercial kidney/inner-ear components. Only the explicitly named Palmar aponeurosis.l and .r surfaces were selected from the official archive. They are palmar fascia, not those excluded assets. Both repository rights/credits and selected-object identities were inspected before integration. No kidney/inner-ear geometry, templates, scripts, definitions, materials or textures are redistributed.

An official Blender 3.6.23 portable runtime was downloaded from download.blender.org and checked against its published SHA-256 (e3296eba7eab32c2e5182459ec7614af32224eee2bd32c9d0a08ffd751c54f3b). It was run with factory startup, background mode and auto-execution disabled to read selected mesh datablocks using our script. Source template scripts were not run. Converted supplement: 2 meshes, 68,448 bytes, CC BY-SA 4.0, separately attributed. A rigid similarity registration uses four existing atlas mesh centers; residuals are retained in the manifest and independent alignment review remains pending. Source meshes are not relabelled clinically validated.

[NIH 3D terms](https://3d.nih.gov/terms) require reviewing the license on each entry; no blanket public-domain assumption is valid. No suitable individually verified palmar asset was selected there, and no NIH model was integrated.

All original source caches and the Blender tool remain in ignored model-sources/tools directories. Distributable assets contain the separate BodyParts3D and palmar-supplement manifests, attribution, license notices and output hashes. Combined credits: app/public/models/ATTRIBUTION.txt. Model compatibility: indexed glTF 2.0, position/normal buffers, no external textures. No independent anatomical completeness/clinical certification is claimed.

The 32 entrance/catalogue previews are original Three.js renders of these reviewed local GLBs. scripts/render-atlas-previews.mjs records canonical members, image hashes and license references in public/previews/manifest.json; its ATTRIBUTION.txt supplies credits. No ANARUNY image or AnatomyLearning code, model, brand asset or definition is distributed. ANATOMYLEARNING_ANALYSIS.md separates visual references/public vendor claims from the unavailable live CDP investigation. Its rendering engine remains unknown.
